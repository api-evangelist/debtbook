For most government accounting teams implementing GASB 96 , implementation starts the same way: a folder of contracts from different departments, half auto-renewing, none with an obvious interest rate, and an audit deadline eight months out. When this phase stalls, the consequences show up as audit findings, restated financials, and year-end scrambles. Most GASB 96 resources explain what the standard requires; this guide covers how to actually implement it, phase by phase, from kickoff to first audit.
