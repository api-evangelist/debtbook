There is a workforce that sits at the financial foundation of every American community: the government finance and treasury professional. They are often small teams, sometimes a department of one, managing the cash, debt, investments, and compliance obligations of cities, counties, school districts, and public universities. Their work funds the roads we drive on, the schools our children attend, and the emergency services we depend on.
