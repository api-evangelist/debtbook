Stacy Lassiter, who leads finance and treasury for Mobile County, Alabama, estimates he spends “probably 90% of [the] day tracking, measuring, and managing” to land on an end-of-day cash position. He does most of that work by hand: recording every deposit, recording every disbursement, then manually calculating where the day lands and what he projects for tomorrow.
